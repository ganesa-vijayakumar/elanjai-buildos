SCHEDULE - B (Specifications)
1. Basement & Concrete works
a) Basement level of building will be 3'0" from the existing NGL.
b) Car parking will be 1'6" from NGL.
c) RCC 1:1½:3 in Column, beams, Lintel and Roof slab 5" Machine jelly.
d) 3" thick for loft (RCC)
2. Brick Work
a) 9" Brick work in CM 1:5 using well burnt stock brick in plinth beam above to roof slab bottom.
b) Inner partition 4.5" Brick work in CM 1:5 using well burnt stock brick in roof slab bottom.
c) Parapet Wall 4.5" Brick work in CM 1:5 using well burnt stock brick in terrace floor.
3. Plastering
a) Plastering towelling, under side of slab, beam columns etc., in CM 1:3, ½" thick finished smooth with sponges.
b) Plastering interior faces of walls and ceiling with CM 1:4, ¾" thick finished smooth with sponge true to plumb.
c) Plastering outer faces of walls with CM 1:4, ¾" thick finished smooth with sponge true to plumb.
4. Flooring & tiles
a) Flooring of all rooms will be of Vitrified Tile (4'x2') @ rate of Rs.50/sq.ft.
b) Toilet flooring and wall tiles (7'0"ht) will be joint free Ceramic tiles of any size with a basic rate of Rs.45/sq.ft. And the floor should be anti-skid tiles with a basic rate of Rs.40/sq.ft.
c) Staircase flooring will be of anti-skid tiles with a basic rate of Rs.40/sq.ft.
5. Iron & Steel works (Grills)
a) M.S. material grills to window with a weight of 1.5 kg/Sq.ft
b) Flats used shall be 10mm square rod.
c) All windows & ventilators will be provided with grills.
6. Carpentry & Joinery
a) Main door frame shall be First class Teak Wood shall be 5"x4" in size.
b) Bed room door frame shall be 3"x2½" in size.
c) Bed room door shall be First quality country woods frames with flush shutters.
d) Toilet doors will be of PVC doors.
e) Window frames and shutters open type or sliding will be UPVC.
f) Ventilator frames 2"x2" in size.
g) All hinges and fittings for internal doors will be of stainless steel.
7. Painting
a) One coat of white cement and two coats of exterior emulsion (Ace) paint for outside walls.
b) Two Coats of Putty a coat of primer and two coats of emulsion paint (Tractor) over for inside wall & two coat ceiling white surface.
c) Two coats of synthetic enamel paint over one coat of primer for grills.
8. Electrical
a) The wiring shall be carried out with PVC insulated copper wires of Orbit or similar brand.
b) Power supply for Air conditioner shall be wired with 4 sq.mm copper wire and provided with switch sockets and plugs of 30 amps rating.
d) GM, Orbit modular switches will be used.
e) Telephone, TV point and AC point will be provided for all the bedrooms.
f) Inverter line for a light and a fan in each room and for a light in toilets will be provided.
g) Toilet will have one 15amp & one 5amp plug point.
ELECTRICAL PROVISIONS:
Hall: Light - 4 nos, Fan - 2 nos, 5amps Socket - 5 nos, Ceiling Spot lights - 4 nos, AC provision

Bedrooms: Light - 2 nos, Fan - 1 no, 5amps Socket - 2 nos (Light n Fan Two way - 1 no each), AC provision, TV - 1 no at 2 master bedrooms, Foot Lamp - 1no at 2 master bedrooms

9. Plumbing
a) Hot water lines will be of CPVC pipes with a hot water connection for toilet.
b) European water closets of Parryware Indus make and other ceramic fittings will also be of Parryware Indus make shall be used and drain connection shall be through raised floor.
c) All PVC pipes will be of Ashirvad or Finolex make.
d) Bath rooms to have 3 in 1 wall mixer with a normal shower and a hand shower.
e) Toilet to have health faucet.
